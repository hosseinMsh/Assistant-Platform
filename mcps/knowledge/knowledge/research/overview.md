# Research Domain Overview

## Research Administration

### Vice Presidency for Research and Technology

The Vice Presidency for Research and Technology (معاونت پژوهشی و فناوری) oversees all research activities at Sharif University of Technology.

### Key Officials
- **Vice President for Research and Technology**: Mohammad Reza Movahhedy
- **Website**: https://research.sharif.ir
- **Office of Research Affairs**: Main research coordination office

### Core Responsibilities

The Vice Presidency oversees:

1. **Research Policy Development**: Setting strategic research directions and priorities
2. **Research Funding**: Management of research grants and funds
3. **Research Ethics**: Ensuring compliance with ethical standards
4. **Research Infrastructure**: Maintenance and development of research facilities
5. **Technology Transfer**: Support for commercialization of research results
6. **Industry Collaboration**: Facilitating partnerships between university and industry
7. **Research Evaluation**: Assessment of research performance and impact
8. **Researcher Support**: Services and support for faculty researchers

## Research Institutes

Sharif University hosts 8 major research institutes, each with specialized focus areas:

### 1. Advanced Communications Research Institute (ACRI)
- **URL**: http://acri.sharif.ir
- **Focus**: Communications systems, wireless technologies, signal processing
- **Research Areas**: 5G/6G networks, optical communications, satellite communications

### 2. Electronics Research Institute (ERI)
- **URL**: http://eri.sharif.ir
- **Focus**: Electronic circuits, embedded systems, nano-electronics
- **Research Areas**: VLSI design, sensor technologies, power electronics

### 3. Institute for Convergence Science and Technology (ICST)
- **URL**: https://icst.sharif.ir
- **Focus**: Interdisciplinary research at science boundaries
- **Research Areas**: Convergence of physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering

### 4. Institute of Transportation Studies & Research (ITSR)
- **Focus**: Transportation systems, traffic engineering, intelligent transportation
- **Research Areas**: Traffic management, public transit, transportation policy

### 5. Research Institute of Science, Technology & Industry Policy (RISTIP)
- **URL**: https://spri.sharif.ir
- **Focus**: Science and technology policy, innovation economics, technology assessment
- **Research Areas**: Technology forecasting, policy analysis, innovation studies

### 6. Sharif Advanced Technologies Incubator (SATI)
- **URL**: https://sati.sharif.ir
- **Focus**: Technology incubation, startup support, entrepreneurship
- **Services**: Business planning, mentorship, funding access, prototype development

### 7. Sharif Energy Research Institute (SERI)
- **URL**: http://seri.sharif.ir
- **Focus**: Energy systems, renewable energy, energy efficiency
- **Research Areas**: Solar energy, wind energy, smart grids, energy storage

### 8. Sharif Institute of Economic & Industrial Studies (SIEIS)
- **URL**: http://sieis.sharif.ir
- **Focus**: Economic analysis, industrial strategy, economic policy
- **Research Areas**: Industrial economics, regional development, economic forecasting
