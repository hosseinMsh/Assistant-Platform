# University Overview

## Identity

Sharif University of Technology (SUT) is the highest ranked technological university in the Islamic Republic of Iran. It was established in 1966 (1344 Solar Hijri) with the aim of training and providing specialized personnel needed in the country at high scientific levels.

### Official Names

- **Persian name**: دانشگاه صنعتی شریف
- **English name**: Sharif University of Technology
- **Acronym**: SUT
- **Type**: Public technological university

### Location

- **Main Campus**: Tehran, Iran - Azadi Avenue, Tarasht neighborhood (near Azadi Tower)
- **International Campus**: Kish Island - Kish International Campus (established 2009)
- **Tehran International Campus**: tic.sharif.ir

### Key Statistics

- **Founded**: 1966
- **Students**: Approximately 10,500 (including ~10,000 on main campus)
- **Faculty Members**: Approximately 475
  - 38% Professors
  - 22% Associate Professors
  - 36% Assistant Professors
- **Alumni**: Approximately 73,000 graduates
- **Patents**: 54+ registered patents
- **Research Articles**: ~4,087 research articles published

### Institutional Identity

Sharif University of Technology has earned a respected reputation and rank among institutes of higher learning in the Middle East. It is a member of:
- International Association of Universities (IAU)
- International Center for Theoretical Physics (ICTP)
- Asian University Presidents Forum (AUPF)
- Federation of the Universities of the Islamic World (FUIW)
- Third World Academy of Sciences (TWAS)

## Organizational Structure

The university is led by the University President, who oversees five Vice Presidencies:

1. **Vice President for Academic Affairs** (Navid Arjmand) - Oversees all academic programs, undergraduate and graduate education, and academic support services
2. **Vice President for Research and Technology** (Mohammad Reza Movahhedy) - Oversees research activities, research institutes, and technology transfer
3. **Vice President for Administration and Finance** (Mohammad Vesal) - Oversees financial administration, construction, and support services
4. **Vice President for Student Affairs** (Amir Norani) - Oversees student welfare, housing, health services, and campus life
5. **Vice President for Cultural Affairs** (Mohammad Fakharzadeh) - Oversees cultural events, student organizations, and artistic activities

The Vice Presidencies are supported by various directorates and offices, including:
- Department Chairs (for each of the 13 departments)
- Directors of research institutes and centers
- Deans of various offices (under VPAA)
- Directors of cultural centers and student organizations

## Academic Levels

The university provides educational services at three levels:

- **Undergraduate (B.Sc.)**: 16 programs across all departments
- **Graduate (M.Sc.)**: 80 programs
- **Doctoral (Ph.D.)**: 20 programs
- **International Students**: 15 programs (main campus and Kish International Campus)

The university operates on a semester system:
- **Fall Semester**: Mid-September to mid-January
- **Spring Semester**: Early February to May

## Mission and Vision

Sharif University of Technology seeks its vision of creating, disseminating, and applying knowledge in a fully dynamic learning environment. This point of view has led to improving the quality of education and research, providing services to society, and solving its problems to the best of its ability. With more than 50 years of experience, it is proud that with the help of God Almighty and the efforts of efficient and experienced professors, prominent researchers, and hardworking experts and employees, as well as the use of educational facilities and well-equipped laboratories, it has been able to train its talented students to the level of the most efficient scientific bodies, and so offering the country's technology to the society.
