# Library Domain Overview

## Central Library

### Library Administration

The Central Library of Sharif University of Technology is the primary information hub for the university community, responsible for supplying and providing information in science and technology to support lectures and research undertaken at the various departments.

### Core Services

#### 1. Collections and Resources
- **Print Collections**: Scientific books, journals, and reference materials in engineering, sciences, and humanities
- **Digital Collections**: E-books, academic databases, and digital resources
- **Special Collections**: Rare books, historical documents, and unique materials
- **Theses and Dissertations**: Collection of Sharif University graduate research

#### 2. Electronic Resources
- **Academic Databases**: Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink
- **E-book Collections**: thousands of electronic books in engineering and sciences
- **Journal Subscriptions**: Key engineering and science journals
- **Data Resources**: Engineering standards, datasets, and specialized resources

#### 3. Research Support Services
- **Literature Search**: Professional assistance with finding relevant research
- **Database Training**: Workshops on using academic databases effectively
- **Citation Management**: Support for reference management tools (EndNote, Mendeley, Zotero)
- **Research Impact Analysis**: Citation analysis and journal metrics
- **Systematic Reviews**: Support for systematic review methodology

#### 3. Access Services
- **Book Lending**: Loan periods and renewal policies
- **Reserve Materials**: Course reserve materials (textbooks, readings)
- **Interlibrary Loan**: Borrowing from other libraries
- **Document Delivery**: Article and chapter delivery services

#### 4. User Services
- **Reference Desk**: Professional research assistance
- **Circulation Desk**: Check-out and return of materials
- **Interlibrary Loan Desk**: ILL request processing
- **Technology Loans**: Laptops, calculators, and other equipment

#### 5. Digital Services
- **Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC)**: Search the library collection
- **E-resource Access**: Remote access to electronic resources
- **Digital Repository**: Access to Sharif University's research output
- **Virtual Library Tour**: Online tour of library facilities
- **Mobile App**: Library services on mobile devices

#### 6. Physical Facilities
- **Study Rooms**: Individual and group study spaces
- **Reading Rooms**: Quiet reading areas
- **Computer Labs**: Access to computers and printers
- **Group Study Rooms**: Collaborative study spaces
- **Computer Classrooms**: Instructional technology-equipped rooms

### Membership and Borrowing

#### Borrowing Privileges
- **Undergraduate Students**: Up to 10 books, 2-week loan period
- **Graduate Students**: Up to 20 books, longer loan periods
- **Faculty Members**: Up to 30 books, semester-long loans
- **Staff**: Up to 10 books, defined loan periods
- **Community Members**: Limited borrowing with registration

#### Renewal and Returns
- **Renewal Policy**: One renewal per item if not requested by others
- **Return Locations**: Main library and departmental branch locations
- **Overdue Fines**: Small fines for overdue materials
- **Lost Item Replacement**: Cost of replacement plus processing fee

### Interlibrary Loan (ILL)

#### ILL Services
- **Borrowing from Other Libraries**: Request materials not in Sharif collection
- **Lending to Other Libraries**: Share Sharif materials with other institutions
- **Processing Time**: Typically 1-3 weeks for requests
- **Loan Period**: Varies by lending library policies
- **Cost**: May include borrowing fees from lending libraries

#### Request Process
1. **Search Catalog**: Check if material is available at Sharif
2. **Submit ILL Request**: Online form through library website
3. **Wait for Processing**: Library reviews and submits request
4. **Notification**: Notification when material arrives
5. **Pickup and Use**: Collect material and use according to policies
6. **Return**: Return by due date to library
