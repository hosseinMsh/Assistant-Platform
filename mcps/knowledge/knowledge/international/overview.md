# International Domain Overview

## International Affairs Office

### Office Structure

The International Affairs Office (دفتر امور بین المللی) is responsible for developing the university's strategy for promoting its international outlook and global profile.

### Key Officials and Websites
- **International Affairs Office Website**: http://ia.sharif.ir
- **Chancellor of International Campus, Kish**: https://en.sharif.ir/international
- **Vice President for Academic Affairs**: https://vpaa.sharif.ir
- **Kish International Campus**: http://www.kish.ac.ir

### Core Responsibilities

The International Affairs Office oversees:

1. **International Strategy**: Developing and implementing the university's internationalization strategy
2. **Bilateral Agreements**: Managing cooperation agreements with international institutions
3. **Student Exchange**: Coordinating student exchange programs
4. **Faculty Exchange**: Supporting faculty mobility and collaboration
5. **International Programs**: Developing and managing international academic programs
6. **Global Rankings**: Monitoring and improving university global rankings
7. **International Collaboration**: Facilitating partnerships between Sharif University and international institutions
8. **Visiting Students**: Managing visiting student programs
9. **Scholarships**: Information and coordination of scholarship opportunities
10. **International Events**: Organizing and supporting international conferences and events

## International Programs

### Student Exchange Programs

#### 1. Outgoing Exchange
- **Eligibility**: Currently enrolled Sharif University students
- **Duration**: One or two semesters
- **Partner Institutions**: Partner universities worldwide
- **Credit Transfer**: Credits earned abroad transfer to Sharif University
- **Application**: Through the International Affairs Office
- **Support**: Pre-departure orientation, course approval, reintegration support

#### 2. Incoming Exchange
- **Eligibility**: Students from partner institutions
- **Duration**: One or two semesters
- **Host Department**: Relevant academic department at Sharif University
- **Credit Transfer**: Credits transferred to student's home institution
- **Application**: Through partner institution or directly to International Affairs
- **Support**: Orientation, academic advising, housing assistance

### Joint and Double Degree Programs

#### 1. Joint Degree Programs
- **Structure**: Collaboration between Sharif University and partner institution
- **Duration**: Extended program spanning both institutions
- **Degree**: Joint degree from both institutions
- **Fields**: Select engineering and science programs
- **Application**: Through partner institution or Sharif University

#### 2. Double Degree Programs
- **Structure**: Students enrolled at both institutions
- **Duration**: Typically 4-5 years
- **Degree**: Two separate degrees, one from each institution
- **Fields**: Limited to specific programs with high demand
- **Application**: Competitive selection process

### Credit Transfer and Course Equivalency

#### Course Equivalency Process
1. **Pre-approval**: Required before studying abroad
2. **Course Description**: Syllabus and learning outcomes submission
3. **Faculty Evaluation**: Department faculty review and approval
4. **Credit Transfer**: Equivalent credit assignment
5. **Transcript Recording**: Official recording on student transcript

#### Equivalency Requirements
- Course content similarity (minimum 80% overlap)
- Learning outcomes alignment
- Assessment method compatibility
- Credit unit compatibility (typically 1:1 ratio)
- Faculty approval from both institutions

## Kish International Campus

### Campus Overview

The Kish International Campus of Sharif University of Technology (SUT) was officially established on Kish Island in 2009.

### Campus Features
- **Location**: Kish Island, Iran (free trade zone)
- **Capacity**: Limited number of students (both international and domestic)
- **Status**: Free trade zone benefits (reduced taxes, special regulations)
- **Connection**: Linked to main Tehran campus academically

### Admission Process

#### For Domestic Students
1. **National Examination**: Konkor results may be considered
2. **University Entrance**: Separate application process
3. **Required Documents**: Academic transcripts, identification, photographs

#### For International Students
1. **Direct Application**: Through Kish Campus website or International Affairs
2. **Educational Background**: Equivalent to Iranian high school diploma (for undergraduate) or Bachelor's degree (for graduate)
3. **Language Proficiency**: Persian or English depending on program
4. **Required Documents**:
   - Academic transcripts and certificates
   - Passport copy
   - Personal statement
   - Letters of recommendation
   - Language proficiency results
   - Financial documentation

### Programs Offered
- Undergraduate programs in various engineering and science fields
- English-taught options available
- Cultural integration programs
- Short-term summer programs

### Campus Life
- **Housing**: On-campus and off-campus options
- **Facilities**: Modern laboratories, classrooms, and student services
- **Cultural Activities**: Integration of Iranian and international student culture
- **Location Benefits**: Kish Island's status as a free trade zone

## International Collaborations

### Bilateral Agreements

#### Agreement Types
- **Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs)**: General cooperation framework
- **Student Exchange Agreements**: Formal exchange program terms
- **Joint Degree Agreements**: Joint or double degree program terms
- **Research Collaboration Agreements**: Joint research project terms
- **Faculty Exchange Agreements**: Visiting professor and researcher terms

#### Partner Institutions
- Sharif University maintains agreements with institutions worldwide
- Partners include universities in Europe, Asia, North America, and the Middle East
- Agreement scope varies: exchanges, joint research, joint degrees, faculty exchanges

#### Agreement Management
- **Centralized Management**: Through International Affairs Office
- **Department-Level Agreements**: Some departments maintain independent agreements
- **Regular Review**: Annual review and update of agreements
- **Compliance**: Ensuring adherence to agreement terms

### Research Collaboration

#### Collaboration Types
- **Joint Research Projects**: Co-funded research with international partners
- **Co-supervision**: Joint doctoral supervision
- **Staff Exchange**: Visiting researchers and professors
- **Student Mobility**: Graduate student exchanges
- **Equipment Sharing**: Shared use of research facilities

#### Funding Collaborations
- **Joint Grant Applications**: International funding bodies
- **Co-funding**: Shared funding from multiple sources
- **Funding Pass-through**: One institution hosts the grant
