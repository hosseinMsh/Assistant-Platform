# Education Domain Overview

## Academic Levels and Programs

Sharif University of Technology provides educational services at three main levels:

### Undergraduate (Bachelor of Science - B.Sc.)
- **Total Programs**: 16 degree programs
- **Fields of Study**: All engineering disciplines, sciences, and management
- **Admission**: Limited to top 5% of students who pass the national entrance examination administered by the Ministry of Culture and Higher Education
- **Duration**: Typically 4 years (160-190 units depending on the department)
- **Degree Requirements**: Completion of required courses, credits, and either a final project or thesis depending on the program

### Graduate (Master of Science - M.Sc.)
- **Total Programs**: 80 programs across all departments
- **Fields of Study**: All engineering and science disciplines, plus specialized programs
- **Admission**: Based on entrance examination scores, GPA, and department-specific requirements
- **Duration**: Typically 2 years (32-36 units)
- **Degree Requirements**: Completion of core courses, elective courses, and a thesis or comprehensive exam

### Doctoral (Ph.D.)
- **Total Programs**: 20 programs
- **Fields of Study**: Specialized research areas across all departments
- **Admission**: Based on M.Sc. performance, entrance examination, research proposal, and interview
- **Duration**: Typically 3-5 years
- **Degree Requirements**: Completion of required courses, passing comprehensive/doctoral exams, and dissertation defense

### International Students
- **Programs**: 15 programs across various fields
- **Admission**: Separate process for international applicants
- **Campuses**: Main campus in Tehran and Kish International Campus
- **Support**: Dedicated services through the International Affairs Office

### Key Educational Concepts

#### Course Selection (انتخاب واحد)
- Process through which students select their courses for the upcoming semester
- Typically occurs during the first weeks of each semester
- Students must complete course selection within specified deadlines
- System: https://edu.sharif.edu/list/courses

#### Registration (ثبت نام)
- Official academic enrollment process
- Occurs at the beginning of each academic year/semester
- Required for all students each semester
- Managed through the educational system

#### Examinations (امتحانات)
- Conducted at the end of each semester
- Include mid-term and final exams
- Managed by the Academic Affairs Office
- Schedule published through the educational system

#### Grades (نمرات)
- Evaluated on a letter grade system (A, B, C, D, F) or percentage system
- GPA calculated based on course grades and credit units
- Grade verification available through the educational system

#### Graduation (فراغت از تحصیل)
- Process of completing all degree requirements
- Application required through the educational system
- Includes thesis/dissertation submission (for graduate levels)
- Academic clearance needed before graduation

#### Academic Calendar (تقویم آموزشی)
- **Fall Semester**: Mid-September to mid-January
- **Spring Semester**: Early February to May
- **Winter Break**: Approximately late December to early February
- **Summer Break**: Approximately late May to early September
- Key dates published annually at: https://ac.sharif.edu/calendar/

#### Academic Regulations (قوانین و مقررات آموزشی)
- Govern all academic matters including:
  - Course registration and selection
  - Examination rules and procedures
  - Grading system and GPA calculation
  - Academic probation and dismissal
  - Graduation requirements
  - Student rights and responsibilities
- Available at: https://ac.sharif.edu/rules/

## Academic Calendar Details

The university academic year is divided into two main semesters:

### Fall Semester
- **Start**: Mid-September (around September 17-22)
- **Start of Classes**: 17-22 Shahrivar (Solar Hijri)
- **Mid-semester Break**: Approximately 50 days after start
- **Final Examinations**: Late December (around Bahman 25-29)
- **End**: Mid-January (around January 17-22)

### Spring Semester
- **Start**: Early February (around February 18-19)
- **Start of Classes**: 4-5 Farvardin (Solar Hijri)
- **Final Examinations**: Late May/early June (around Khordad 10-15)
- **End**: Early May to mid-May

### Key Academic Dates (Sample - varies by year)
- **Registration Period**: First 3 weeks of semester
- **Course Add/Drop**: First 2 weeks of semester
- **Mid-term Exams**: Approximately 8-10 weeks into semester
- **Final Exams**: Last 2 weeks of semester
- **Grade Submission**: 1-2 weeks after final exams
- **Grade Verification**: 1-2 weeks after grade submission
- **Semester Break**: Between fall and spring semesters

## Course Registration Process

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Prepare Study Plan**: Students consult with academic advisors
2. **Check Eligibility**: Ensure all prerequisites are met
3. **Access System**: Log into Samaneh Amozesh (https://edu.sharif.edu)
4. **Select Courses**: Choose courses from available list
5. **Submit Registration**: Submit course selection before deadline
6. **Confirm Registration**: Verify registration is complete
7. **Print Schedule**: Obtain printed course schedule

### Important Deadlines (per semester)
- **Registration Opens**: 17 Shahrivar (start of fall) / 4 Farvardin (start of spring)
- **Registration Deadline**: 22 Shahrivar / 19 Farvardin
- **Add/Drop Period**: First 2 weeks of classes
- **Final Registration**: 2-3 weeks after semester starts
- **Withdrawal from Course**: Up to 10 Dey (December) for fall semester

## Examination System

### Examination Types

| Examination Type | Timing | Description |
|----------------|--------|-------------|
| **Mid-term Examination** | 8-10 weeks into semester | Assesses understanding of first half of course content |
| **Final Examination** | Last 2 weeks of semester | Comprehensive assessment of entire course |
| **Quizzes** | Throughout semester | Short assessments, may be announced or unannounced |
| **Final Project/Presentation** | Last 2-3 weeks of semester | For courses with practical components |
| **Comprehensive Examination** | End of graduate programs | Comprehensive test of knowledge in field |
| **Doctoral Dissertation Defense** | Final stage of Ph.D. | Defense of dissertation research |

### Examination Rules

- Students must attend examinations on scheduled dates and times
- Absence without valid excuse results in "Absent" grade
- Make-up examinations only for documented medical or personal emergencies
- Cheating or academic dishonesty results in severe penalties including course failure or dismissal
- Examination schedules published at least 2 weeks in advance
- Room assignments and seating plans published 3-5 days before exams

### Grade Calculation

Final course grade typically based on:
- **Mid-term Exam**: 30-40% of final grade
- **Final Exam**: 40-50% of final grade
- **Quizzes/Assignments**: 10-30% of final grade
- **Projects/Presentations**: 0-20% of final grade (course-dependent)

Grade conversion (typical):
- A: 90-100%
- B: 80-89%
- C: 70-79%
- D: 60-69%
- F: Below 60%

### Grade Point Average (GPA)

- Calculated each semester
- Based on course grades and credit units
- Cumulative GPA tracks overall performance
- Minimum GPA required for good standing (typically 2.0/4.0 or 10/20)
- Probation for students falling below minimum GPA

## Graduation Process

### Degree Requirements

#### Bachelor's Degree
1. Complete all required courses (minimum credit units)
2. Complete elective courses as specified by department
3. Pass all required examinations
4. Complete graduation thesis or final project (if required by department)
5. Maintain minimum cumulative GPA (typically 2.0/4.0)
6. Clear all financial and administrative obligations

#### Master's Degree
1. Complete required core courses (minimum credit units)
2. Complete elective courses
3. Pass comprehensive examination (if required by program)
4. Complete and defend thesis (for thesis-based programs)
5. Or pass final examination (for non-thesis programs)
6. Maintain minimum cumulative GPA
7. Clear all financial and administrative obligations

#### Doctoral Degree
1. Complete required coursework (if applicable to program)
2. Pass comprehensive/doctoral examinations
3. Conduct original research
4. Write and defend dissertation
5. Publish research (often required in top journals)
6. Maintain minimum cumulative GPA
7. Clear all financial and administrative obligations

### Graduation Application

1. Submit graduation application through the educational system (https://edu.sharif.edu)
2. Pay graduation fees
3. Clear all administrative holds
4. Submit thesis/dissertation to the department
5. Schedule and complete defense (graduate levels)
6. Attend graduation ceremony (held twice yearly)
7. Receive degree certificate and transcript

### Graduation Ceremonies
- Held twice yearly (typically in Tir and Dey months of Solar Hijri)
- Presided over by the University President
- Degree certificates conferred in person
- Family and guests invited
