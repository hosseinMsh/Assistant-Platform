## Document Types

Each knowledge document follows this structure:
1. **Title** (Persian and English where applicable)
2. **Overview**: Summary of the topic
3. **Key Details**: Important facts, procedures, requirements
4. **Related Systems**: Connected university systems
5. **Official Sources**: List of authoritative URLs
6. **Source Documents**: Specific PDFs or documents referenced

## Quality Rules

- All knowledge must be verified against official university sources
- Persian terminology must be preserved with English translations
- No hallucination or invention of facts not in sources
- All statements must have source citations
- Documents must be understandable without opening original websites
- Temporary information must be clearly marked with dates/context
