# Faculty Domain Overview

## Faculty Affairs Administration

### Directorate of Faculty Affairs

The Directorate of Faculty Affairs (Direkate Afrad va Hedayat-e Daneshgah) serves as the central administrative unit responsible for managing all faculty-related matters at Sharif University of Technology.

### Key Officials
- **Director of Faculty Development and Retention**: Zahra Jamshidi
- **Website**: https://facultyaffairs.sharif.ir
- **Persian Website**: https://facultyaffairs.sharif.ir/fa/

### Core Responsibilities

The Directorate oversees the following key areas:

#### 1. Faculty Development and Retention
- Professional development programs for academic staff
- Career progression and promotion pathways
- Retention strategies and incentive programs
- Workload management and balance
- Recognition and awards for excellence

#### 2. Faculty Services
- Professional development opportunities
- Research support and collaboration facilitation
- Administrative services for academic personnel
- Grant application support
- Conference participation support

#### 3. Academic Units Management
- Department administration oversight
- Faculty assignment to departments and courses
- Cross-departmental assignments
- Resource allocation within departments

## Faculty Development Programs

### Professional Development Opportunities

#### 1. Rank Promotion
- **Assistant Professor → Associate Professor**: Based on performance, publications, and contributions
- **Associate Professor → Professor**: Based on research output, supervision, and administrative contributions
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Research publications, student supervision, conference participation, patents, and administrative service

#### 2. Research Grants and Funding
- Internal research grants for faculty members
- External funding support and facilitation
- Grant writing workshops and support
- Post-grant reporting and evaluation

#### 3. International Collaboration
- Visiting professor opportunities
- Joint research projects with international institutions
- Conference travel grants
- Sabbatical leave opportunities

#### 4. Teaching Development
- Pedagogy workshops and training
- Curriculum development support
- Instructional technology training
- Classroom observation and feedback

#### 5. Administrative Training
- Department chair training
- Committee service training
- Resource management workshops
- Policy and procedures training

## Faculty Services

### Research Support
- **Grant Facilitation**: Assistance with research proposal development and submission
- **Funding Opportunities**: Information about internal and external funding sources
- **Research Administration**: Support for research project management
- **Intellectual Property**: Patent filing and technology transfer support

### Administrative Services
- **Personnel Records**: Maintenance of faculty employment and academic records
- **Payroll and Benefits**: Salary, benefits, and retirement administration
- **Leave Management**: Vacation, sick leave, and other absence management
- **Professional Development Tracking**: Record of training and development activities

### Teaching Support
- **Course Assignment**: Assignment of faculty to courses
- **Curriculum Development**: Support for course design and revision
- **Instructional Technology**: Training on teaching technologies and platforms
- **Classroom Resources**: Support for classroom setup and equipment

### Professional Recognition
- **Teaching Awards**: Annual teaching excellence awards
- **Research Awards**: Recognition for research contributions
- **Service Awards**: Recognition for administrative and community service
- **Merit-based Honors**: Annual recognition of outstanding performance

## Faculty Rights and Responsibilities

### Rights
- Academic freedom in research and teaching
- Participation in governance and decision-making
- Professional development opportunities
- Fair evaluation and promotion processes
- Protection against arbitrary dismissal
- Access to university resources

### Responsibilities
- Teaching and instructional duties
- Research and scholarly activity
- Student supervision and mentoring
- Service to the university and department
- Participation in committees and working groups
- adherence to ethical guidelines
- Maintenance of professional standards
