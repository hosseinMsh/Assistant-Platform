# Admission Domain Overview

## University Admission System

### Admission Administration

The University Admission System (سامانه پذیرش) is managed through the official admission portal and oversees all student admission processes.

### Key Officials and Websites
- **Admission System Website**: https://admission.edu.sharif.edu
- **International Affairs Office**: http://ia.sharif.ir
- **Vice President for Academic Affairs**: https://vpaa.sharif.ir
- **Main Website (Persian)**: https://www.sharif.ir/fa/web/international
- **Main Website (English)**: https://en.sharif.ir/admission

### Admission Phases

#### 1. Undergraduate Admission
- **Exam**: National entrance examination (Konkur), administered by the Ministry of Culture and Higher Education
- **Eligibility**: Top 5% of examinees
- **Application**: Through the national system and university portal
- **Quota**: Limited number of students per program
- **Timeline**: Typically begins after national exam results (around summer)

#### 2. Graduate Admission (M.Sc.)
- **Based on**: M.Sc. entrance examination, GPA, and department-specific requirements
- **Application**: Through the graduate studies office
- **Timeline**: Typically twice per year (fall and spring admissions)

#### 3. Doctoral Admission (Ph.D.)
- **Based on**: M.Sc. performance, entrance examination, research proposal, and interview
- **Application**: Through the graduate studies office and research affairs
- **Timeline**: Rolling admissions with specific cycles

#### 3. International Student Admission
- **Process**: Separate application for international applicants
- **Options**: 
  - Undergraduate: Based on secondary school performance and entrance exam
  - Graduate: Based on previous degree performance and research proposal
  - Exchange programs: Through bilateral agreements
- **Support**: International Affairs Office (http://ia.sharif.ir)
- **Kish Campus**: Separate admission for Kish International Campus

## Undergraduate Admission Details

### Application Process

1. **National Examination (Konkur)**
   - Administered nationwide by Ministry of Culture and Higher Education
   - Required for all Iranian applicants
   - Ranking based on scores determines eligibility

2. **University Application**
   - Online application through: https://admission.edu.sharif.edu
   - Required documents:
     - Secondary school certificate and transcript
     - National examination scores
     - Identity documents
     - Recent photographs
   - Application fee payment

3. **Selection Process**
   - Rank order based on national exam scores
   - Department and program preferences
   - Quota allocation per department
   - Final acceptance notification

## Graduate Admission (M.Sc. and Ph.D.)

### Master's Degree Admission

#### Application Requirements
1. **Bachelor's Degree**: From an accredited institution
2. **Minimum GPA**: Typically 13/20 or 2.5/4.0 (varies by department)
3. **M.Sc. Entrance Examination**: Department-specific or national
4. **Required Documents**:
   - Bachelor's degree certificate and transcript
   - transcripts from all previous education
   - Statement of purpose
   - Letters of recommendation (typically 2-3)
   - Recent photographs
   - Identity documents
   - Research proposal (for some programs)

2. **Selection Criteria**
   - Entrance examination scores (weight: ~50%)
   - Bachelor's GPA (weight: ~30%)
   - Statement of purpose and letters of recommendation (weight: ~20%)
   - Department-specific additional requirements

3. **Program Duration**
   - Typically 2-3 years
   - Thesis-based or non-thesis options
   - Comprehensive examination requirement (for some programs)

#### Ph.D. Degree Admission

#### Application Requirements
1. **Master's Degree**: In a related field from an accredited institution
2. **Minimum GPA**: Typically 15/20 or 3.0/4.0 (varies by department)
3. **Ph.D. Entrance Examination**: Department-specific
4. **Required Documents**:
   - Master's degree certificate and transcript
   - transcripts from all previous education
   - Detailed research proposal
   - Statement of purpose
   - Letters of recommendation (typically 3)
   - Recent publications (if any)
   - Recent photographs
   - Identity documents
   - Curriculum vitae

5. **Selection Criteria**
   - Entrance examination scores (weight: ~40%)
   - Master's GPA (weight: ~30%)
   - Research proposal quality (weight: ~20%)
   - Letters of recommendation and interviews (weight: ~10%)
   - Previous research experience and publications

6. **Program Duration**
   - Typically 3-5 years
   - Comprehensive/doctoral examinations required
   - Dissertation/thesis required
   - Publication expectations (often 1-2 papers in indexed journals)

### International Student Admission

#### Undergraduate International Applicants
1. **Secondary School Certificate**: Equivalent to Iranian high school diploma
2. **Entrance Examination**: May be required or waived based on country of origin
3. **Language Proficiency**: Persian or English depending on program
4. **Required Documents**:
   - Certified secondary school transcripts
   - University entrance examination results (if applicable)
   - Language proficiency test results (Persian: TestDaF equivalent; English: TOEFL/IELTS)
   - Personal statement
   - Letters of recommendation
   - Identity documents (passport, birth certificate)
   - Equivalence certificate from Ministry of Education (for non-Iranian degrees)

#### Graduate International Applicants
1. **Master's Degree**: Bachelor's degree with excellent performance
2. **Ph.D. Degree**: Master's degree with research experience
3. **Language Proficiency**: Required for program language
4. **Required Documents**:
   - Bachelor's/Master's transcripts and certificates
   - Research proposal
   - Language proficiency results
   - Letters of recommendation
   - Statement of purpose
   - CV/research summary
   - Equivalence documentation

#### Special Pathways
- **Exchange Students**: Through bilateral university agreements
- **Joint Degree Programs**: Co-degree programs with international institutions
- **Short-term Visiting Students**: Semester or year-long visits

## Admission Timeline

### Academic Year Timeline

#### Fall Semester (Starting September)
- **May-June**: Announcement of admission opportunities
- **July-August**: Application period for new students
- **August**: Application review and selection
- **September**: Orientation and registration
- **September 17-22**: Start of Fall semester classes

#### Spring Semester (Starting February)
- **October-November**: Announcement of spring admission
- **December-January**: Application period
- **January**: Application review and selection
- **February 18-19**: Start of Spring semester classes

#### Continuous Admissions
- Some programs accept applications year-round
- Rolling admissions for certain graduate programs
- International student admissions on going basis

## Important Application Documents

### Common Required Documents
1. **Academic Transcripts**: Official transcripts from all previous institutions
2. **Degree Certificates**: High school diploma (undergraduate) or Bachelor's degree (graduate)
3. **Identification**: National ID card or passport
4. **Photographs**: Recent passport-style photos
5. **Personal Statement**: Study goals and motivation
6. **Letters of Recommendation**: Typically 2-3 academic or professional references
7. **Standardized Test Scores**: Depending on program (entrance exams, language tests)
8. **Financial Documentation**: For scholarship and visa purposes
9. **Medical Certificate**: Health clearance (sometimes required)
10. **Equivalence Certificate**: For degrees obtained outside Iran (through Ministry of Education)

### Document Authentication
- All documents must be official and stamped
- International documents require Ministry of Education equivalence
- Documents in foreign languages must be translated into Persian
- Notarization may be required for certain documents

## Application Fees

### Fee Structure
- **Undergraduate**: Approximately 500,000 - 1,000,000 Toman (varies yearly)
- **Master's**: Approximately 800,000 - 1,500,000 Toman (varies yearly)
- **Ph.D.**: Approximately 1,000,000 - 2,000,000 Toman (varies yearly)
- **International Students**: Different fee structure (often higher, but with scholarship options)

### Payment Methods
- Through the university's financial system
- Bank transfer
- Online payment portal
- Payment receipt required for application processing

### Fee Waivers
- Available for exceptional financial need
- Based on documented hardship
- Application through separate form
- Limited number of waivers available annually

## Admission Results and Notifications

### Notification Process
- **Email**: Official acceptance notifications sent to applicant email
- **Student Portal**: Status check through MySharif Portal (https://my.sharif.edu/login)
- **Text Message**: SMS notifications for Iranian applicants
- **Official Letter**: Mail acceptance letters for some categories

### Acceptance Timeline
- **Undergraduate**: Typically 4-6 weeks after application deadline
- **Master's**: Typically 2-4 weeks after application deadline
- **Ph.D.**: Typically 4-8 weeks after application deadline
- **International**: Varies by applicant country and program

### After Acceptance
1. **Pay Registration Fee**: To secure admission spot
2. **Submit Additional Documents**: Required final documents
3. **Register for Courses**: During registration period
4. **Complete Pre-enrollment**: Health, housing, and other preparations
5. **Attend Orientation**: New student orientation sessions
