# Materials Science and Engineering

**Department Website:** https://mse.sharif.ir

## Overview

Materials Science and Engineering at Sharif University of Technology is one of the university's 10 engineering departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Materials Science and Engineering
- M.Sc. in Materials Science and Engineering
- Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering

## Research Focus
- Nanomaterials and nanotechnology (related to COE)
- Metallic materials and processing
- Ceramic and composite materials
- Materials characterization and testing

## Related Centers
- Center of Excellence in Nanostructures (COE)
