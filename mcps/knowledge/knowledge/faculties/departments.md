# Faculties and Departments Domain Overview

## University Structure

Sharif University of Technology comprises 15 academic units: 13 departments and 2 specialized centers that function as academic units. All departments offer Bachelor's (B.Sc.), Master's (M.Sc.), and Doctoral (Ph.D.) degree programs.

### Organizational Hierarchy
```
University
├─ Vice Presidencies (5)
│   ├─ Academic Affairs
│   ├─ Research and Technology
│   ├─ Administration and Finance
│   ├─ Student Affairs
│   └─ Cultural Affairs
├─ 15 Academic Units (Departments/Centers)
│   └─ Faculty Members
└─ Supporting Units
```

## Academic Programs by Department

### Engineering Departments (10)
Each engineering department offers:
- **Bachelor's (B.Sc.)**: 4-year program, approximately 160-190 credit units
- **Master's (M.Sc.)**: 2-year program, 32-36 credit units + thesis or comprehensive exam
- **Doctoral (Ph.D.)**: 3-5 year program, dissertation required

### Science and Humanities Departments (5)
Each department offers programs specific to their field with similar degree structures.

### Double Counting Note
The student population numbers shown above reflect shared enrollments, as some students take courses across departments, and the total university student body is approximately 10,500 (not the sum of all departmental student counts).
