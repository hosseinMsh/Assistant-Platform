# Mechanical Engineering

**Department Website:** https://www.mech.sharif.ir

## Overview

Mechanical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology is one of the university's 10 engineering departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering
- M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering
- Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering

## Research Focus
- Thermodynamics and energy conversion (related to Center of Excellence in Energy Conversion - CEEC)
- Design, robotics, and automation (related to CEDRA)
- Aerospace systems and structures
- Manufacturing and production

## Related Centers
- Center of Excellence in Energy Conversion (CEEC)
- Center of Excellence in Design, Robotics, and Automation (CEDRA)
