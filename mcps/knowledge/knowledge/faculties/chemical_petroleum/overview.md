### Academic Structure
The faculty is organized into specialized areas within chemical and petroleum engineering:

#### Core Discipline Areas
- **Chemical Engineering**: Process design, transport phenomena, thermodynamics, separation processes
- **Petroleum Engineering**: Reservoir engineering, drilling, production, refining, petrochemicals
- **Mass Transfer**: Absorption, distillation, extraction, leaching
- **Heat Transfer**: Energy integration, heat exchangers, thermal systems
- **Reaction Engineering**: Chemical reaction kinetics, reactor design, catalysis
- **Plant Design**: Process flow diagrams, PFD/P&ID, equipment design
- **Environmental Engineering**: Waste treatment, pollution control, green chemistry
- **Petrochemical Industries**: Polymer production, benzene/toluene/xylene, fertilizers

#### Laboratory Facilities
- Unit operations laboratory (distillation, extraction, absorption)
- Heat transfer laboratory
- Reaction engineering laboratory
- Mass transfer laboratory
- Computer-aided process design laboratory
- Petroleum properties laboratory
- Pilot plant facilities for process simulation

### Educational Programs

#### Undergraduate (B.Sc.) in Chemical Engineering
- **Duration**: 4 years (160-190 credit units)
- **Core Curriculum**:
  - Mass Transfer Operations
  - Heat Transfer
  - Fluid Mechanics
  - Chemical Reaction Engineering
  - Thermodynamics
  - Process Simulation (Aspen HYSYS, Aspen Plus)
  - Plant Design and Economics
  - Unit Operations Laboratory
  - Mathematics for Chemical Engineers
  - Chemistry Foundation
  - Petroleum Refining Introduction
- **Senior Design Project**: Capstone design project in chemical plant design
- **Practical Training**: Laboratory courses and 6-month industry internship

#### Undergraduate (B.Sc.) in Petroleum Engineering
- **Duration**: 4 years (160-190 credit units)
- **Core Curriculum**:
