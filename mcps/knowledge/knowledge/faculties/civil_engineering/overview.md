# Civil Engineering

**Department Website:** https://civil.sharif.ir

## Overview

Civil Engineering at Sharif University of Technology is one of the university's 10 engineering departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Civil Engineering
- M.Sc. in Civil Engineering
- Ph.D. in Civil Engineering

## Research Focus
- Structural engineering
- Earthquake engineering (related to Center of Excellence in Structures and Earthquake Engineering - CESEE)
- Transportation and infrastructure
- Construction and materials

## Related Centers
- Center of Excellence in Structures and Earthquake Engineering (CESEE)
