# Industrial Engineering

**Department Website:** https://www.ie.sharif.ir

## Overview

Industrial Engineering at Sharif University of Technology is one of the university's 10 engineering departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Industrial Engineering
- M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering
- Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering

## Research Focus
- Operations research and optimization
- Production and manufacturing systems
- Quality control and management
- Supply chain and logistics
- Economic and industrial studies (related to SIEIS)

## Related Centers
- Sharif Institute of Economic & Industrial Studies (SIEIS)
