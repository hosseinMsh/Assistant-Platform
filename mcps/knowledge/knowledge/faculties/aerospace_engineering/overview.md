### Academic Structure
The faculty is organized into specialized areas within aerospace engineering:

#### Core Discipline Areas
- **Aerodynamics**: Study of air flow around aircraft and spacecraft
- **Propulsion**: Engine and rocket propulsion systems
- **Structures**: Aircraft and spacecraft structural analysis
- **Flight Mechanics**: Aircraft performance and stability
- **Astrodynamics**: Orbital mechanics and spacecraft trajectory analysis
- **Avionics**: Aircraft electronics and control systems

#### Laboratory Facilities
- Wind tunnel facilities for aerodynamic testing
- Propulsion test facilities
- Structural testing laboratories
- Flight simulation laboratories
- Materials testing facilities

### Educational Programs

#### Undergraduate (B.Sc.)
- **Duration**: 4 years (160-190 credit units)
- **Core Curriculum**: 
  - Aerospace Materials
  - Fluid Mechanics
  - Aerospace Propulsion
  - Aerospace Structures
  - Flight Dynamics and Control
  - Aerospace Design Projects
- **Design Projects**: Senior design project in aerospace system design
- **Practical Training**: Laboratory courses and industry internships

#### Graduate (M.Sc.)
- **Duration**: 2 years (32-36 credit units)
- **Specializations**: 
  - Aerodynamics and Fluid Dynamics
  - Propulsion and Combustion
  - Aerospace Structures
  - Flight Mechanics and Control
  - Space Systems
- **Thesis Requirement**: Research thesis in specialized area

#### Doctoral (Ph.D.)
- **Duration**: 3-5 years
- **Requirements**: 
  - Advanced coursework
  - Comprehensive examinations
  - Original dissertation research
  - Publications in indexed journals
  - Defense of dissertation
