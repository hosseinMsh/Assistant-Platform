# Chemistry

## Overview

Chemistry is one of the university's 2 science departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Chemistry
- M.Sc. in Chemistry
- Ph.D. in Chemistry

## Research Focus
- Organic and inorganic chemistry
- Physical and analytical chemistry
- Materials chemistry
- Chemical engineering applications

## Related Areas
- Chemical and Petroleum Engineering (interdisciplinary collaboration)
