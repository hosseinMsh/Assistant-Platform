# Electrical Engineering

**Department Website:** https://www.ee.sharif.ir

## Overview

Electrical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology is one of the university's 10 engineering departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering
- M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering
- Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering

## Research Focus
- Communications and signal processing
- Power systems and control
- Electronics and circuits
- Microelectronics

## Related Centers and Institutes
- Advanced Communications Research Institute (ACRI)
- Electronics Research Institute (ERI)
- Center of Excellence in Multi-Access Communication Systems (CEMACS)
- Center of Excellence in Power System Management and Control (CEPSMC)
