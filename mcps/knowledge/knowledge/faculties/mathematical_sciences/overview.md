# Mathematical Sciences

**Department Website:** http://math.sharif.edu

## Overview

Mathematical Sciences is one of the university's 2 science departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs in mathematics and statistics.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Mathematical Sciences
- M.Sc. in Mathematical Sciences
- Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences

## Research Focus
- Pure mathematics (algebra, analysis, geometry)
- Applied mathematics
- Statistics and probability
- Discrete structures and computing (related to Center of Excellence in Discrete Structures and Computing - CEDSC)

## Related Centers
- Center of Excellence in Discrete Structures and Computing (CEDSC)
