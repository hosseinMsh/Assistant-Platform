# Physics

**Department Website:** http://physics.sharif.ir

## Overview

Physics is one of the university's 2 science departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Physics
- M.Sc. in Physics
- Ph.D. in Physics

## Research Focus
- Theoretical physics
- Experimental physics
- Condensed matter physics (related to Center of Excellence in Computer Systems and Condensed Matter - CSCM)
- Nanostructures (related to Center of Excellence in Nanostructures - COE)

## Related Centers
- Center of Excellence in Nanostructures (COE)
- Center of Excellence in Computer Systems and Condensed Matter (CSCM)
