# Energy Engineering

**Department Website:** http://energy.sharif.ir

## Overview

Energy Engineering at Sharif University of Technology is one of the university's 10 engineering departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Energy Engineering
- M.Sc. in Energy Engineering
- Ph.D. in Energy Engineering

## Research Focus
- Renewable energy systems
- Energy conversion (related to CEEC)
- Power system management (related to CEPSMC)
- Energy policy and economics (related to SIEIS)

## Related Centers
- Center of Excellence in Energy Conversion (CEEC)
- Center of Excellence in Power System Management and Control (CEPSMC)
- Sharif Energy Research Institute (SERI)
