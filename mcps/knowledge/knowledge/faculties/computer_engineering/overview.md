# Computer Engineering

**Department Website:** https://ce.sharif.ir

## Overview

Computer Engineering at Sharif University of Technology is one of the university's 10 engineering departments, offering B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs.

## Programs
- B.Sc. in Computer Engineering
- M.Sc. in Computer Engineering
- Ph.D. in Computer Engineering

## Research Focus
- Computer systems and architecture
- Software engineering
- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Information security and cryptography (related to Center of Excellence in Information Security and Cryptography)
- Computing and condensed matter (related to Center of Excellence in Computer Systems and Condensed Matter - CSCM)

## Related Centers
- Center of Excellence in Information Security and Cryptography
- Center of Excellence in Computer Systems and Condensed Matter (CSCM)
- Center of Excellence in Design, Robotics, and Automation (CEDRA)
