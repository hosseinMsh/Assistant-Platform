## Housing and Dormitory Services

### Accommodation Affairs Office

The Accommodation Affairs Office operates under the Student Affairs Deputy and plays a pivotal role in providing housing solutions for the student population.

### Dormitory Facilities

**On-Campus Housing**:
- **2-Bedroom Suites**: Available for students with study spaces
- **Dedicated Housing**: Separate facilities for male and female students
- **Study Spaces**: Integrated study areas within dormitory units
- **Capacity**: Designed to accommodate a significant portion of the student population

### Application Process
1. **Application Submission**: Through the Student Affairs Portal
2. **Eligibility**: Based on academic standing and financial need
3. **Assignment**: Based on availability, gender, and academic level
4. **Payment**: Semesterly housing fees
5. **Contract**: Signed housing agreement

### Housing Fees
- Semesterly rates vary by facility type
- Discounts available for certain categories
- Payment through the university financial system
- Refund policies for early withdrawal

### Off-Campus Housing Support
- Information and resources about off-campus options
- Roommate matching services
- University partnerships with nearby housing complexes

## Health Services

### Smart Health Cloud System

Sharif University of Technology is committed to fostering the physical, mental, and emotional well-being of its students through the Smart Health Cloud System—a technology-driven platform designed to empower individuals to take charge of their health through proactive, self-care approaches.

### Key Features
- **Personalized Fitness Programs**: Tailored exercise plans based on individual needs
- **Customized Nutrition Planning**: Diet plans considering health goals and restrictions
- **Mental Health Support**: Access to psychological resources and support
- **Data Analytics**: Integration of health data for personalized recommendations
- **User Feedback**: Continuous improvement based on student input

### Access
- Available through the MySharif Portal (https://my.sharif.edu/login)
- Requires Sharif ID authentication
- 24/7 access to health resources
- Mobile-friendly platform

### Counseling Center

The Counselling Center at Sharif University of Technology is dedicated to promoting and safeguarding the mental well-being of students.

### Services Offered
- **Individual Counseling**: One-on-one psychological support sessions
- **Group Counseling**: Group therapy and support sessions
- **Psychotherapy**: Professional psychotherapy services
- **Workshops**: Mental health awareness and skill-building workshops
- **Crisis Intervention**: Immediate support for psychological crises

### Recognition
- Recognized as one of the most effective and reputable student counselling services among Iranian universities
- Team of highly qualified specialists
- Evidence-based practices
- Student-centered approach

### Access to Services
- Through the Counseling Center website: https://counseling.sharif.ir
- In-person appointments at the center
- Emergency crisis support
- Referral to external mental health professionals when needed

## Student Welfare

### Welfare Office

The Students' Welfare Office, operating under the Student Affairs Administration, serves as a vital link between the University and the Welfare Fund for Students.

### Key Functions
- **Financial Coordination**: Coordination with the National Welfare Fund
- **Social Support Programs**: Assistance programs for students in need
- **Program Coordination**: Management of various student support programs
- **Staff and Faculty Support**: Assistance for staff and faculty using campus facilities

### Welfare Programs
- **Scholarship Support**: Assistance with scholarship applications and maintenance
- **Housing Assistance**: Support for students needing housing aid
- **Financial Hardship**: Emergency financial assistance
- **Social Services**: Connection to social support networks

### Application Process
1. Register through the Welfare Office
2. Submit required documentation
3. Eligibility assessment based on financial need and academic standing
4. Approval and benefit assignment
5. Ongoing reporting and eligibility maintenance

### Contact
- Welfare Office: https://society.portal.farhangi.sharif.edu/
